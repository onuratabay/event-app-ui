Ewent is a Turkish-language community app for adults aged 18 and over. Members discover and organize real-world activities, share availability, and communicate in event rooms and direct chats.

New memberships are subject to reference and approval requirements. Please use the dedicated, approved review account entered in the App Review sign-in fields to access the member features. The account must remain approved and accessible throughout review.

Suggested review flow:
1. Tap “Giriş yap” on the welcome screen and sign in with the supplied review credentials.
2. Open “Keşfet” to browse events, and “Harita” to view events on the map.
3. Open an event to inspect its time, location and participation information.
4. Tap the center W button to open “Oluştur”, which offers event creation and availability sharing.
5. Open “Odalar” to access the account’s available conversations and event rooms.

Location permission is requested for nearby/location features. Contacts permission is used to find existing members for reference requests. Camera/photo access is used for profile and event images. These permissions can be declined. The iOS advertising tracking request can also be declined.

Account deletion is available in the app under Profil > Ayarlar > Hesabı sil > Hesabımı sil.
